# Marketplace and workspaces
Mode: Operate

## Direction contract
THESIS: A produce catalogue connecting farmers and buyers through clear availability and a staff-assisted inquiry workflow.

OWN-WORLD: Forest green navigation/action states, ivory ground, flat white produce panels, DM Sans, compact farm data, real crop photography. The signature is a consistent volume/handling fact strip shared by catalogue, details and tables.

STORY: Buyers see available produce, narrow supply, inspect harvest details and submit an inquiry. Farmers publish supply and record baseline history. Staff select a farmer and assist.

FIRST VIEWPORT: A 232px desktop rail beside a top utility bar. A page title precedes a wide green-and-field introduction; crop tabs and search/filter controls lead directly into a three-column produce grid. Mobile uses a compact header, accessible drawer and single-column cards on small phones.

FORM: Produce catalogue, seventh grounded candidate, seed 82a8479f. User explicitly delegated style choice. Code-led app build; no approved comp.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
